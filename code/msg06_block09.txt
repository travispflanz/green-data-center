        <span class="badge badge-statute">Enacted Federal Law</span>
        <h3>Germany: Energy Efficiency Act (EnEfG § 11)</h3>
        <p>Section 11 of the EnEfG establishes Europe's strictest data center performance regime. In addition to mandating that new facilities achieve a PUE of 1.2 or lower, operators must register with the Federal Platform for Waste Heat (<em>BAFA</em>) and export up to 20% of their thermal output into local heating networks.</p>
        <p><a href="https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/enefg/__11.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener" style="color: var(--primary);">Read Official Federal Law (Gesetze im Internet) ↗</a></p>
      </article>

      <article class="card">
        <span class="badge badge-statute">Grid Connection Policy</span>
        <h3>Ireland: CRU Large Energy User Policy</h3>
        <p>Under the Commission for Regulation of Utilities framework, hyperscalers cannot simply procure existing national clean energy. They must demonstrate 80% additionality by financing new renewable generation within Ireland and install on-site storage (BESS) equal to 100% of their import capacity.</p>
        <p><a href="https://www.cru.ie" target="_blank" rel="noopener" style="color: var(--primary);">Inspect CRU Grid Rulings ↗</a></p>
