        <span class="badge badge-solar">Commercial Nuclear Restart</span>
        <h3>Crane Clean Energy Center (Three Mile Island Unit 1)</h3>
        <p>In September 2024, Constellation Energy signed a 20-year Power Purchase Agreement with Microsoft to restart the 835 MW Unit 1 pressurized water reactor. The $1.6 billion overhaul targets commercial operation between 2027 and 2028, supported by proposed interconnection transfers from retiring fossil plants to avoid regional transmission queues.</p>

        <span class="badge badge-statute">Regulatory Precedent</span>
        <h3>AWS / Talen Susquehanna (FERC Docket ER24-2172)</h3>
        <p>In November 2024, FERC rejected an amended Interconnection Service Agreement that would have expanded Amazon's behind-the-meter compute load at the Susquehanna nuclear plant from 300 MW to 480 MW. The ruling highlights regulatory concerns over shifting transmission infrastructure costs onto retail ratepayers.</p>
