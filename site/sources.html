Primary statutory acts & directives

German Federal Energy Efficiency Act (EnEfG § 11)

Statute · gesetze-im-internet.de · cited on Regulation, Cooling

Statutory mandates for data center efficiency: PUE thresholds (≤1.2 new builds), renewable quotas (100% by 2027), waste heat reuse (10–20%), and the 27°C inlet temperature requirement.

gesetze-im-internet.de/enefg/__11.html ↗

European Union Recast Energy Efficiency Directive (Directive (EU) 2023/1791)

Statute · EUR-Lex · cited on Regulation

Delegated regulation establishing the European database and energy rating scheme for data centers ≥ 500 kW, with mandatory PUE/WUE/temperature/waste-heat auditing.

eur-lex.europa.eu — Directive (EU) 2023/1791 ↗

Commission for Regulation of Utilities (CRU Ireland) — LEU Directives

Regulatory policy · cru.ie · cited on Regulation

Grid connection policies requiring 80% renewable additionality from new Irish projects and 100% on-site dispatchable backup or storage for Large Energy Users.

cru.ie — Commission for Regulation of Utilities ↗

Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA Singapore) — Green DC Roadmap & GMDC:2024

Regulatory policy · imda.gov.sg · cited on Regulation

Statutory standards for tropical data centers: PUE cap of 1.25, DC-CFA2 green energy requirements (≥50% green pathways), and BCA-IMDA Green Mark Platinum.

imda.gov.sg — IMDA Singapore ↗
