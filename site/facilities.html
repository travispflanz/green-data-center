Detailed Facility Case Studies

100% Solar Baseload

Moro Hub (Dubai, United Arab Emirates)

Certified by Guinness World Records as the world's largest solar-powered data center. Located directly within the Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum Solar Park, the facility operates with an initial 6.3 MW phase scaling toward 100 MW of compute capacity, avoiding evaporative water cooling in a severe desert environment.

Zero Potable Evaporation

Microsoft Next-Gen AI Facilities (Phoenix & Wisconsin)

Microsoft has deployed Direct-to-Chip (DLC) liquid cooling cold plates connected to massive exterior dry coolers (utilizing 172 20-foot fans per building). Once charged with fluid during initial commissioning, the system operates continuously with zero evaporative water consumption, saving over 125 million liters of water per facility annually.

Geothermal Baseload

Verne Global (Keflavík, Iceland)

Powered entirely by Iceland's dual-source geothermal and hydroelectric transmission grid. The facility takes advantage of the subarctic maritime climate to use 100% ambient free-air economization year-round, eliminating mechanical refrigerants and evaporative towers completely.
